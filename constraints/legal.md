# Constraint: Legal

> Constraints are **immutable during execution**. AI never violates them. Changing one requires an ADR + human approval (Level 0).

## Non-negotiables
- All dependencies must have compatible licenses (see LICENSING)
- No scraping/ingestion of content that violates ToS
- Terms and Privacy must exist before public launch
